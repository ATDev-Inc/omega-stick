Omega Stick home

First flash and recovery

Write Omega Stick firmware onto a board from your browser. No software to install.

Use this page only if you need to

  • Use this page for a brand new board, to install a new version (the current release does not update over USB), or for a stick that will not connect to the console at all.
  • If the console offers a firmware update for your stick, use that instead. It keeps your calibration and settings.
  • This erases everything on the board, including calibration and saved settings. You will calibrate again after the first start.

This browser cannot talk to USB devices

Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop (Windows, macOS, Linux, or ChromeOS). Firefox, Safari, and phone browsers do not support the Web Serial feature this page needs.

Step 1: put the board in download mode

A blank board usually enters download mode by itself, but doing these steps does no harm. A board that already has firmware needs them: its USB port is busy acting as a mouse, so you have to ask the chip to wait for new firmware.

Never plug or unplug the sensor cable (the small Qwiic connector) while the board has power. Those connectors do not connect power in a safe order and can damage the board. Unplug USB first, change cables, then plug USB back in.

The board has two small buttons, labelled BOOT and RESET. Their positions depend on the board.

  1. Plug the board into your computer with a USB cable that carries data (some cables only charge).
  2. Press and hold BOOT.
  3. While still holding BOOT, press and release RESET.
  4. Let go of BOOT.

No RESET button, or that did not work? Unplug the board, hold BOOT, plug it in while holding, then let go.

The board will look inactive: no mouse movement and usually no light. That is normal in download mode. Your computer may play its USB connect sound and the board will show up as a serial device (often named “USB JTAG/serial debug unit”).

Step 2: install

Firmware to install: checking the latest version...

This browser does not support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. This page must be opened over HTTPS to install firmware.
  1. Choose Connect and install. Your browser shows a list of serial ports.
  2. Pick the one for your board (see below if the list is empty), then choose Connect.
  3. The installer offers to erase the device first. Leave that ticked for a first flash.
  4. Wait for it to finish. It takes about a minute. Do not unplug the board.
  5. Unplug the board and plug it back in. It now starts normally.

If something goes wrong

  • The port list is empty. Repeat step 1 and try a different USB cable or port. Most failures are charge-only cables.
  • Linux: your user may need permission for the device (usually the dialout group), then log out and in again.
  • Windows: if the board does not appear after entering download mode, try another USB port. Close other programs that may be holding the port, such as a serial monitor.
  • It stopped partway. The board is not harmed. Put it in download mode again and repeat.
  • Still stuck? Open an issue and say which board and operating system you use.

The installer is ESP Web Tools, loaded from a fixed version. It writes the same firmware file that is attached to each release.
