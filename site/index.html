Omega Stick

An open, low-force joystick for people who cannot use a standard mouse.

Calibrate, tune, or update your stick

The console connects to a stick that is already running Omega Stick firmware. Use it to calibrate, change settings, and install new firmware. Updates from the console keep your calibration.

Open the console

First install or recovery

A new board, or a stick that no longer connects to the console, needs the firmware written from scratch. This erases the calibration and settings.
